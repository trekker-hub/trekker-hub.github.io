About

Who I am

Computer Engineer, hardware builder, and occasional mentor to teenagers who are better at debugging than I expected.

I'm a Computer Engineering student at George Mason University graduating Spring 2026 with a 3.95 GPA. My work sits where hardware meets intelligent systems — cycle-accurate HDL design, FPGA-based DSP pipelines, and formal verification for safety-critical environments.

At Fermi National Accelerator Laboratory, I worked on NPU development and quantum computing instrumentation. The kind of environment where "close enough" isn't a valid tolerance. I liked that.

I transferred to GMU after earning two Associate of Science degrees at NOVA. Outside of engineering, I run a weekly mentorship program for teenagers at a community center — the best way to really understand something is to explain it to a 16-year-old who will immediately ask why.
